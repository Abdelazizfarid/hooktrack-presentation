Layer 2 · Device integrity

Detecting rooted & tampered phones

A rooted phone can hide a fake-GPS app from every location check. So the device itself is verified first, on every launch and on every check-in.

Root & jailbreak signatures

su binaries, Magisk, SuperSU, test-keys builds, writable system partition.

Hardware-backed attestation

Google Play Integrity / Apple DeviceCheck verdicts validated on the server, not on the phone.

Runtime protection

Emulator, debugger, hooking frameworks (Frida, Xposed) and app repackaging are detected.

Rooted device → the app runs in locked mode: no check-ins, and the ERP shows the device as untrusted.

Duration · sync to ERP

1 · Start of day

Rep opens the app. Device integrity scan runs. Today's plan downloads with store locations, geofence radius and tasks.

2 · Navigate

Turn-by-turn to the first store. Location is sampled in the background and streamed to the ERP for live tracking.

3 · Geofenced check-in

Check-in button unlocks only inside the store radius (e.g. 100 m) with a genuine, un-spoofed fix. Timestamp, coordinates and accuracy are sealed into the visit.

4 · In-store tasks

Shelf photos (camera only, no gallery upload), stock count, order capture, competitor survey. Photos are geo- and time-stamped.

5 · Check-out & sync

Visit duration is computed. Everything syncs to the ERP, offline-first, and appears on the manager's map within seconds.
