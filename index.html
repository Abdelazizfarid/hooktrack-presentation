Security first

A visit only counts if the location is real

Before a check-in or an order is accepted, the app proves two things about the phone.

🛰️ The location is genuine

Not injected by a fake-GPS app, not replayed, not teleported.

📱 The device is trustworthy

Not rooted, not an emulator, not tampered with.

Fail either check → the visit is flagged, the order is held, and the manager is alerted in real time.

Layer 2 · device integrity

Detecting rooted & tampered phones

A rooted phone can hide a fake-GPS app from every location check. So the device itself is verified first — on every launch and every check-in.

Root & jailbreak signatures

su binaries, Magisk, SuperSU, test-keys builds, writable system partition.

Hardware-backed attestation

Google Play Integrity / Apple DeviceCheck verdicts validated on the server, not on the phone.

Runtime protection

Emulator, debugger, hooking frameworks (Frida, Xposed) and app repackaging are detected.

Rooted device → the app runs in locked mode: no check-ins, no orders, and the ERP marks the device untrusted.

1 · Start of day

Rep opens the app. Device integrity scan runs. Today's plan downloads: customers, geofence radius, price list per customer, active promotions.

2 · Navigate

Turn-by-turn to the next customer. Location streams to the ERP in the background for live tracking.

3 · Geofenced check-in

The check-in button unlocks only inside the store radius (e.g. 100 m) with a genuine, un-spoofed fix. Timestamp, coordinates and accuracy are sealed into the visit.

4 · Shelf check

Stock count per SKU (3-in-1 boxes, loose coffee bags), shelf photos (camera only, geo-stamped), competitor prices, expiry check.

5 · Place the order

Pick products and quantities. The right price list loads automatically; promotions and free goods are added by the app. Credit limit and stock are checked before sending.

6 · Collect & notes

Record cash or cheque collection against open invoices, returns, and visit notes.

7 · Check-out & sync

Visit duration is computed. Visit, photos and order sync to the ERP — offline-first — and appear on the manager's map within seconds.
